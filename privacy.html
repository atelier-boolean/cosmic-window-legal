Cosmic Window

Privacy Policy

Last updated 30 October 2026

Cosmic Window shows NASA's pictures, videos, recordings and data. You don't need an account, and most of what you do stays on your device. This policy explains what does leave it, and why.

Who we are

Cosmic Window is made by Atelier Boolean, which is responsible for your data under this policy. Write to us at atelier.boolean@gmail.com with any question or request about your data.

What stays on your device

There's no account and we don't run our own servers, so we never receive or store your data. These stay on your device until you clear them in Settings or remove the app:

  • your favorites, and the recordings you saved;
  • your recent searches and where you stopped in each recording;
  • pictures and data the app keeps to work offline;
  • your NASA API key, if you add one: stored encrypted, sent only to NASA's API (api.nasa.gov), and never shown or logged.

Ads

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. Never on the Astronomy Picture of the Day, whose pictures belong to their photographers.

To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google receives your device's advertising ID (or Apple's IDFA), your IP address, basic device and app details, and how you interact with the ads.

  • Your choice. Where the law requires it (the EU, the UK, Switzerland and some US states), the app asks first through Google's consent form. Ads are personalised only if you agree; otherwise Google shows limited ads, not based on your interests.
  • On iPhone and iPad, Apple asks whether the app may track you. If you say no, the advertising ID isn't shared.
  • Change your mind any time in Settings → Privacy choices, or in your device's settings (reset or turn off the advertising ID; on iOS, Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).

Google's own use of this data is covered by How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Services the app connects to

Like any website, each of these sees your IP address and basic details of your device when the app connects:

  • NASA's services, for pictures, videos, recordings, data and maps.
  • Google, to check you're online (gstatic.com), and through Firebase, to read the app's settings. Firebase gives the app an installation ID, used only for that.
  • YouTube, when you play a video embedded from it.
  • Google AdMob, for ads, as above.

Your photos

The app asks to add to your photo library only when you save something, and it never reads your library.

Why we may process your data

  • Your consent, for personalised ads and, on iOS, tracking. You can withdraw it at any time, as above.
  • Our legitimate interest in keeping the app free with limited ads, preventing ad fraud, and changing the app's settings without an update.

Where your data goes

Google may process data in the United States and other countries. Transfers from the EU rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework and the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

Children

The app isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data. If you're under the age of digital consent where you live (16 in Romania), ask a parent before agreeing to personalised ads.

Your rights

Under the GDPR, and similar laws elsewhere, you can ask to access, correct, delete or move your data, object to or limit how it's used, and withdraw consent. Since we don't hold your data, most of it is on your device, where you can delete it, or with Google, through Settings → Privacy choices. For anything else, write to us.

In some US states you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of your data for ads, also in Settings → Privacy choices.

You can complain to your data protection authority; in Romania, the ANSPDCP.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top. Significant changes will also be shown in the app.
