Delete your data

Last updated 2 November 2026

Cosmic Window has no accounts, and Atelier Boolean doesn't run servers that store your data. What the app keeps is on your device, and you can delete it yourself, at any time.

1. Delete what's on your device

In the app, open Settings → Storage → Clear cached data and confirm. This deletes, at once:

  • your favorites and the recordings saved for offline listening;
  • your recent searches and where you stopped in each recording;
  • NASA data, pictures and map tiles kept to work offline;
  • which version of the terms you accepted (the app asks again).

To remove your NASA API key, use Settings → NASA API Key → Remove. Uninstalling the app deletes all of the above. On Android it deletes the key too; on iPhone and iPad the system keeps the key after the app is removed, so remove it in the app first.

2. Ads and Google

Ads come from Google AdMob, which keeps its own data under the Google Privacy Policy. You can:

  • withdraw consent for personalised ads in Settings → Privacy choices (where it applies);
  • reset or delete your device's advertising ID (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads), or turn off tracking on iOS (Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking);
  • manage or delete your data with Google at Google's data & privacy page.

Uninstalling the app also removes the installation ID that Firebase (Google) uses to send the app its settings.

3. Ask us

For anything else, write to atelier.boolean@gmail.com with the subject "Cosmic Window data request". We hold no data about you on our side, and we'll answer within 30 days.

What's kept

Nothing on our side: there's no copy to keep. Google keeps ad and Firebase data for the periods described in its own policies.
